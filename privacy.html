Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy explains what information BloomLabs (“we,” “us”) collects when you visit our website and play our games, and how it is used. By using the site, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We do not require accounts, and we do not ask you for your name, address, or password to play. We may receive the following:

  • Basic usage data: such as pages visited, device and browser type, approximate location (country or region), and referring website. This is collected by our hosting provider and any analytics tools we use.
  • Information you send us: if you use our contact form, we receive the name, email address, and message you enter. The form is processed by a third-party form service (Web3Forms), which passes your message to us.

Game progress and local storage

Some games save your progress, settings, or high scores using your browser’s local storage. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. Clearing your browser data will erase it.

Cookies and advertising

We may show ads on the site to help cover its costs. Third-party advertising partners, such as Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses data and manage ad personalization at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some parts of the site may not work as expected.

How we use information

  • To run, maintain, and improve the website and games
  • To understand which games are popular
  • To fix bugs and keep the site secure
  • To reply when you contact us

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with service providers who help us run the site (such as hosting, analytics, and advertising), or if required by law.

Children’s privacy

BloomLabs is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

External links

The site may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date above will show when changes were made.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Please reach us through our contact page.
