🗺️ Land and controlsDrag the map or use WASD/arrows to move. Click wild land next to your border to buy it: the price rises as you grow and mountains cost extra. Forest is cleared when bought. Rivers can’t be built on, but farms and docks beside them do better. Click a building to demolish it (half refund). Use x2/x4 to speed up time.
🏠 BuildingsHouse (40g): homes for 5 people. Farm (50g): +6 food a day, +3 more beside the river. Barracks (80g): +5 soldier slots. Market (120g, Castle 2): +8 gold a day. Mine (150g, Castle 2, on a mountain): +12 gold a day. Dock (90g, Castle 2, beside the river): +8 food a day. Tavern (130g, Castle 3): +8 happiness. Watchtower (160g, Castle 3): +3 defense.
👑 CastleUpgrade to Lv2 for 200g and Lv3 for 500g. Each level adds 5 homes, 2 defense and 5 gold a day, and unlocks new buildings.
💰 TaxesEach peasant pays tax% × 0.2 gold a day (10% = 2 gold each). Soldiers cost 1 gold a day. Higher taxes lower happiness by about 2.2 points per 1%.
🌾 FoodPeasants eat 0.5 food a day and soldiers eat 1. Farms and docks produce it: spring ×1, summer ×1.2, autumn ×1.5, winter ×0.4. The store holds up to 250. A full store (4+ food per person) adds happiness and attracts newcomers. If food runs out, families starve and leave. You can sell 30 food for 20 gold.
😊 People and happinessNewcomers join when happiness is above 55 and there are free homes. People leave when it drops below 40, when there are no homes, or in famine. Happiness: 90 minus taxes, +6 well fed, +8 per Tavern (max 16), +8 Fair Laws, −30 famine, −15 overcrowded.
📰 News and eventsThe Royal news log shows what happens each day. Every 50 days a good or bad event strikes (festival, caravan, wedding, gold, plague, drought, fire, warlord). Seasons change every 25 days.
⚔️ Battles and armyBandits attack about every 7 days; stronger defense means fewer attacks. Raiders come as Brutes, Slingers and Riders. Spearmen beat Riders, Archers beat Brutes and Knights beat Slingers (double power against their match). Defense also comes from the castle, towers, allies and Tactics. A rally point (🚩) gathers your soldiers and gives +25% defense.
🤝 NeighborsGifts raise their mood. At mood 20+ you can sign a trade deal (Eastmark +6 food, Goldvale +10 gold, Ironhold +5 gold and +4 defense). At 50+ with trade you can ally, and allies help in battles. Attacking loots gold but sours relations. Hostile neighbors may invade.
🔬 Research and 📜 questsResearch costs gold and gives permanent bonuses. Advisor Aldric gives quests with gold rewards; finish them all to reach Empire.
