About BloomLabs

Free browser games, made for players.

What is BloomLabs?

BloomLabs is a growing collection of browser games you can play right away. There is nothing to download and no account to create. Pick a game, press Start, and play.

Why we made it

We wanted a place where games load fast, work on almost any computer, and don’t get in your way. Every game here is built to be easy to pick up and hard to put down.

What you can expect

  • Free games that run in your browser
  • New games added regularly
  • Games that save your progress on your own device
  • Simple design with minimal clutter
